rechtzeitig

A GitHub Action you start by hand. It opens your website the way a first-time visitor from Germany would and reads your repository. It reports what German and EU law asks for and what is missing.

checked§in time
  • Imprint and privacy policyDDG, GDPR
  • ConsentServers, cookies and banners before consent, TDDDG
  • Contract buttonsCancellation and withdrawal, BGB
  • Security basicsHTTPS, TLS, headers, exposed files, error pages, email spoofing
  • RepositorySBOM, known vulnerabilities, license conflicts, committed secrets

It is free and runs on GitHub's runners.

Start in two minutes

  1. Copy rechtzeitig.yml to .github/workflows/rechtzeitig.yml in your repository.
  2. Open Actions → rechtzeitig → Run workflow, enter your URL and pick the language.
  3. Read the report on the run's summary page.
