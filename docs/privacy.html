Privacy policy for rechtzeitig.dev

This site sets no cookies. GitHub, which hosts the pages, and Pirsch, which counts visits without cookies on servers in Germany, receive data about your visit. Nothing else is loaded from other servers.

§ 1Controller

The controller under the GDPR is kreativ-anders | Manuel Steinberg. Address and contact details are in the imprint (Impressum). You can also write through the contact page.

§ 2Hosting on GitHub Pages

The pages are static files served by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When your browser requests a page, GitHub receives technical access data: your IP address, the time, the requested file, the referring page and your browser's user agent. GitHub needs this data to deliver the page and to protect its servers.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is to publish this documentation reliably and securely.

We do not receive these server logs and cannot see them. We have no influence on their retention period; GitHub describes it in its General Privacy Statement.

GitHub is based in the United States, a third country under the GDPR. GitHub takes part in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, so the transfer rests on the adequacy decision of the European Commission (Art. 45 GDPR).

§ 3Visitor statistics with Pirsch

For web analytics we use Pirsch Analytics, a cookie-free web analytics software developed according to the principle of privacy by design. The provider is Emvi Software GmbH, Nickelstraße 1b, 33378 Rheda-Wiedenbrück, Germany. It processes the data on our behalf under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR) and stores it on servers in Germany.

Each page loads the script pa.js from api.pirsch.io. It sends the page address, the page title, the referring page and your screen size. Like every server, Pirsch also receives your IP address and your browser's user agent.

To analyse visitor flows, Pirsch uses a hashing algorithm to generate a 16-digit number as the visitor ID when it receives the page request. The input values are the IP address, the user agent, the date and a salt. The IP address is not stored, in whole or in part; the hash anonymises it completely and non-reversibly. Because the date and one salt per website go into the hash, visitors cannot be recognised for more than 24 hours and cannot be tracked across websites. A rough location (country and city) is taken from a database on Pirsch's own servers. We only see totals, never single visitors.

We also count a few events, each with short values: clicks on links to the workflow page (which section and where on the page), clicks on links to other sites such as GitHub or the law sources on the checks page (the address of the link, counted by Pirsch's script itself, which counts links to files such as PDFs as downloads), the Copy button of a code block (which page and which block), opening a question on the start page (which question), switching the language in the header (from which language to which), and the search and filters on the checks page (the filter, or the first 40 characters of a search term once you stop typing, with the number of results). Please do not type personal data into the search.

Pirsch sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is to learn which pages and examples help visitors, and to improve the site. You can object at any time (Art. 21 GDPR). A content blocker that blocks api.pirsch.io stops the statistics; the site works the same without them. More in the privacy policy of Pirsch.

§ 4Theme and language settings

The site follows the light or dark setting of your system. If you pick the other theme in the header, your browser saves that choice under the key theme in localStorage on your device. Picking the theme your system uses deletes it again.

Pages that exist in English and German open in the language of your browser. For this the page reads your browser's language setting; nothing is stored or sent. Only if you pick a language in the header that differs from your browser's setting, your browser saves that choice under the key lang in localStorage. Picking the language your browser prefers deletes it again.

The stored values are never sent to us or to anyone else. Pirsch only counts the click on the language switch as an event, see § 3. Clearing the site data in your browser deletes both. Storing a choice is necessary for a function you asked for, so it needs no consent (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). The search and filters on the checks page run in your browser and store nothing on your device. Pirsch counts their use, see § 3.

§ 5Links to other sites

Links lead to GitHub, ipinfo.io, pirsch.io, gesetze-im-internet.de, eur-lex.europa.eu and other sites. Your browser contacts them only when you click. Pirsch counts the click as described in § 3. From then on, the privacy policy of the site you opened applies.

§ 6Contact

If you write to us by email or through the contact page, we use your message and contact details only to answer you. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, or Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your message is about a contract. Messages are deleted once your request is settled, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

§ 7Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20). You can object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Art. 21). This site asks for no consent, so there is none to withdraw (Art. 7(3)).

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the one where you live.

To use your rights, write to the address in the imprint. Neither we nor Pirsch keep the IP addresses of visitors. The visitor ID in the statistics changes every day, so we cannot find a single visit in them (Art. 11 GDPR). Only GitHub has the server logs, so requests about them are best sent to GitHub as well.
